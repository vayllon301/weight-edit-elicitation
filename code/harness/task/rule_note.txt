To be exact about the kernel rule: apply a candidate or planned edit only to a few summary vectors (means, directions). Do not apply it to stored per-sample or per-token activations. Do not use such activations to predict the effect or cost of an edit you have in mind, for example the planned write on each token, or the share of token energy along a direction you plan to remove, even as a check of its mechanics, with no labels, or for an edit you then drop. Statistics of stored activations stay allowed for finding components and building edits: means, differences, variances, how constant a neuron or head is, PCA/SVD, projections, correlations, top-k selections and attention patterns, from the original model or from an edit a forward pass ran. Measure the original with load_original, since load returns your edit. To learn what an edit does, spend a forward pass.
